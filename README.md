☕ Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analytics

This project presents a Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analytics case study focused on understanding sales performance, transaction behaviour, product performance, and opportunities to improve revenue and operational efficiency.

As the data analyst, I analysed the available coffee shop transaction data to uncover meaningful sales trends and translate them into actionable business insights and recommendations.

The analysis focuses on four key areas:

Sales & Transaction Trends — Analyse monthly, daily, and time-of-day sales patterns to understand when sales and transaction activity are highest.
Store & Product Performance — Compare store locations, product categories, and individual products to identify the strongest contributors to overall revenue.
Customer Purchasing Patterns — Analyse transaction quantity and purchasing behaviour to understand customer activity across different periods and days.
Business Growth Opportunities — Use data-driven insights to identify opportunities for improving promotions, staffing, inventory planning, and overall sales performance.

Project Outcome

The project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics and business intelligence workflow, from data preparation and exploratory analysis to dashboard development and strategic recommendations.

The findings provide Bright Coffee Shop with a clearer understanding of sales trends, high-performing products and stores, peak transaction periods, and areas for potential growth. The insights can support better sales planning, inventory management, staffing decisions, and promotional strategies.
